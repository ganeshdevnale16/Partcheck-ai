    Install PartCheck

    iPhone / iPad (Safari)

    1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
    2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. PartCheck appears on your home screen.

    Android (Chrome)

    1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

    Computer (Chrome or Edge)

    1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu.
    2. Choose Install PartCheck AI. It opens in its own window and appears in the Start menu / Applications.
